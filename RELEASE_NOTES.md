# Chinlearn 2.5

- Identical words in different lists now share learning progress. Cards match when their written forms, pronunciation, and full set of meanings agree. Each list keeps its own checkboxes, hints, and examples. Practice across multiple lists schedules a matching word once.
- Extra study follows the usual review schedule, including the spacing between questions for the same word. You can still introduce up to five more new words after reaching your daily limit.
- Turn on **Номера и стрелки** in **Порядок черт** to see where each stroke starts and which direction it takes, including during step-by-step playback.
- The Progress screen opens faster with large libraries, and the handwriting pad captures fast strokes more smoothly.

Export a full library backup in **Настройки** before updating. Install the new APK over your existing app to keep your local data. If you sync across devices, update each device before resuming sync.
